FrameCast Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

FrameCast is a Mac app for recording an iPhone or iPad in a device frame. It is made by Sidwell Labs (“we”). This policy covers the FrameCast app only. The sidwelllabs.com website is separate and is not covered here.

The short version

Your recordings, screenshots, file names and device names never leave your Mac. FrameCast has no accounts and no sign-in.

FrameCast sends two kinds of anonymous data: crash reports and usage counts. Both are on by default. You can turn off each one in FrameCast > Settings > Privacy, and it stops straight away.

We don’t sell this data or use it for advertising.

What stays on your Mac

FrameCast does all of its work on your Mac. It never uploads:

  • your recordings, screenshots or the images you drop in
  • what is on your iPhone or iPad screen, or its sound
  • file names, folder names or paths
  • the names of your iPhone, iPad or Mac
  • your settings or custom device models

FrameCast asks macOS for Camera access (to show an iPhone or iPad connected by USB), Microphone access (to record the device’s sound) and Screen Recording access (for iPhone Mirroring). It uses these only to show and record your device on your Mac.

Crash reports

If FrameCast crashes or stops responding, it sends a report of where it happened so we can fix it. Reports go to Sentry, which stores them for us in the United States.

A crash report contains:

  • where in FrameCast’s code the problem happened (the stack trace), and a short list of what the app did just before
  • the FrameCast version and build
  • the macOS version
  • your Mac’s model (such as Mac16,8) and processor type
  • memory figures, such as how much memory was free
  • your language and region setting
  • a random ID made on your Mac when FrameCast was installed, so reports from the same Mac can be grouped

A crash report does not contain your recordings, screenshots, file names, device names, IP address or location. Your home folder is replaced with ~ in any path in the report, which removes your account name. FrameCast sends a placeholder instead of your IP address, and IP address storage is turned off in our Sentry project.

To stop sending crash reports, turn off Send crash reports in FrameCast > Settings > Privacy. A report saved before you turned it off is thrown away, not sent.

Usage counts

FrameCast counts how often its main features are used, so we know what to improve. The counts go to PostHog, which stores them for us in the United States. This is the complete list of what FrameCast sends:

app_opened
Each time FrameCast opens. Nothing else.
app_installed
The first time FrameCast opens on a Mac. Nothing else.
app_updated
The first time FrameCast opens after an update, with previous_version.
onboarding_finished
The welcome tour ends, with skipped: whether Skip was pressed.
device_connected
The first picture arrives from a device, with source (usb or mirroring) and kind (iphone or ipad).
recording_started
A recording starts, with format (mov, mp4, webm or gif), area (device or view), source and kind.
recording_saved
A recording is saved, with the same details plus duration as a range such as 10–30s, never the exact length.
recording_discarded
The save panel is cancelled, with the same details as recording_started.
recording_failed
A recording can’t be made or saved, with stage (start, finish, convert or save) and the recording_started details when known. Never the error message, which can contain file paths.
screenshot_exported
A framed screenshot is saved or copied, with action (save or copy) and from (live or image).

Each event also carries:

  • a random ID made on your Mac, and a random ID for the current session. These aren’t linked to your name, email or any account, and no profile of you is kept.
  • the FrameCast version and build
  • the macOS version
  • your Mac’s model (such as Mac16,8) and type (such as “Mac”)
  • your screen size
  • your language and time zone
  • whether your Mac is on Wi-Fi

FrameCast sends a placeholder instead of your IP address, and turns off PostHog’s lookup of your location. It doesn’t record clicks, screens or sessions.

To stop sending usage counts, turn off Share anonymous usage data in FrameCast > Settings > Privacy.

Update checks

About once a day, FrameCast checks for a new version by downloading teseife.github.io/FrameCast-releases/appcast.xml, and updates are downloaded from GitHub. These are ordinary web requests, so GitHub sees your IP address, as any website does (see GitHub’s privacy statement). FrameCast doesn’t send anything else with them. You can turn off automatic checks in FrameCast > Settings > Updates.

Feedback

Send Feedback opens the FrameCast feedback board at framecast.userjot.com in your web browser. FrameCast doesn’t send anything to it. Whatever you post there is covered by UserJot’s own terms and privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep crash reports and usage counts only as long as they help us fix and improve FrameCast. Sentry and PostHog delete them after their retention periods for our plans.

Children

FrameCast isn’t made for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If what FrameCast sends changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top before the new version ships, and say so in the release notes.

Contact

For questions about this policy, or to ask us to delete data, email privacy@sidwelllabs.com.
